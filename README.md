# -B-adventure
WIP KubeJS scripts for new untitled Minecraft 1.20.1 adventure modpack

Goals: Shake up the normal play patterns of heavily modded Minecraft, key aspects of which are:
  1. Heavy tech focus and large sprawling tech trees.
  2. Huge bases and focus on staying put, due to automation in crafting and production of resources, which leads to missing large swaths of content in other dimensions, biomes, and structures. (by the time you go for the atm star, you can creative fly and one shot all of most of the dimension mods, so they are pointless).
  3. Material imbalances, which make many mods entire material lines obsolete within the progression (ATM Metals, Refined Obsidian, Silent Gear Tyrian Steel are some big offenders)(Twilight Forest, Aether, Cataclysm, Undergarden materials never see the light of day).
  4. Materials and systems are rarely fully intermingled - Silent Gear almost never works on lots of the modded materials, only 'most' stuff can be melted down with metalworks mods, some mods fully supplant entire other mod material lines.
  5. Adventure based modpacks are typically fully open ended, with minimal questline and almost no direction.

Actions/Solutions: Create a new modpack with a focus on adventure, exploration, and true integration of materials, mobs, bosses, and content.
  1. Custom questline that merges multiple dimension mods and actually rewards and entices working through them simultaneously or consecutively, as opposed to getting strong enough to blitz them all and moving through them at speed.
  2a. Heavily cut back on building material mods, larger focus on backpacks and tents, with the ability to remain almost fully mobile at all times
  2b. No automated resource production or crafting, requiring seeking out materials for low-to-the-ground crafts for gear and tools.
  3/4. Fully inter-laced gear system focusing on Simply Swords - Custom stats for most materials, custom ways to obtain many materials. A progression line that requires play through of most of the major dimensions and/or bosses.
  5. A full and robust quest book, with things from small repeatable tasks (catch fish, kill skeleton, mine ore) to progression tasks that push you further in the quest line (go to nether, get next tier of gear) to major milestones with large rewards (kill dragon, go to every dimension, kill all cataclysm bosses, get to last tier of gear)
